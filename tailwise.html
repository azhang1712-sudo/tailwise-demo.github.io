How matching works

Your score comes only from your answers and each dog's temperament. A dog's age is never part of it, and neither is whether a vet has reviewed the dog or a photo was uploaded. Your ZIP and radius only decide which shelters are in range. When two dogs fit about equally, dogs on a shelter's euthanasia list appear a little earlier.
